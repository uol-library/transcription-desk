19
- To Jug a Hare. -
Cut it into small pieces, baist them with small
pieces of strips of Bacon here & there, season them
with cayenne pepper & salt, put them with an
earthen jug with a little, mace and onions
stuck with cloves, & a bundle of sweet herbs cover
the jug or jar you do it in close that nothing can
get in them set it in a foot of boiling water & three hours
will do it then take it out into a dish & take out
the onions & sweet herbs, & send it to table hot,
if you do not like it larded have it out
- To hash Rabbits -
Cut them up, & put them into a very clean stew pan;
with a little water, &a blade or two of mace, stew
them, till they are sufficiently done then skim off
all the bits, & add half a pint of cream a little
butter, nutmeg, cayenne, & salt.
Chicken and Ham Patties
Skin & mince very fine the breast or white fleshy
parts of a cold chicken and about half the
quantity lean ham or tongue highly flavoured.
Have in a little sauce pan a little good veal gravy
drawn from bones or trimmings, or the jelly of roast
